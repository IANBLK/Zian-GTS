during the early development of Zian GTS. The inspected reference artifact was:

- Project: cobble-gts
- Reference file: cobblegts-neoforge-1.7.0-1.5.2.jar
- Upstream author: xpointfive

upstream permission status as resolved. No upstream-derived portion may be
relicensed merely by applying the Zian GTS MIT notice.

See `docs/LICENSING_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md` for the release gate and affected
